About OpenVerse

OpenPak's own community space: posts, drawings and conversations about the games you play.

One account

Your OpenPak account is your OpenVerse account. There is nothing separate to set up, and no password here: sign-in happens on openpak.org.

Web, app and consoles

Posts made on the web, in the OpenPak app and on Wii U and 3DS are one conversation. A drawing you make here is stored in the console's own format, so it shows up on the consoles too.

Communities

Each game has a community. Follow the ones you play, post in them, and give Yeahs to what you like. Communities and posts follow the community rules and OpenPak's terms.

Privacy and safety

Accounts supervised through OpenPak parental controls can read but not post. Anyone can report a post, and moderators remove what breaks the rules.

Open source

OpenVerse is free software under the AGPL-3.0: github.com/openpak/openverse. Its Wii U and 3DS support builds on Juxtaposition by Pretendo Network (AGPL-3.0), which OpenVerse still relies on for the console protocol.
